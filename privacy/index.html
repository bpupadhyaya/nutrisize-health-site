Privacy Policy

Nutrisize Health · Effective Date: October 26, 2026

1 Who We Are

Nutrisize Health is provided by EqualInformation, LLC.

If you have privacy questions or requests, you may contact us at:
Email: privacy@nutrisize.health or privacy@equalinformation.com
Alternative: nutrisize.universal@gmail.com
Contact page: equalinformation.com

2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Nutrisize Health app (iOS bundle identifier: com.nutrisize.health; Android package name: com.nutrisize.health) and related EqualInformation, LLC pages that link to this policy. Nutrisize Health is available on iOS (App Store), Android (Google Play), and as a web application.

3 Information Nutrisize Health May Access or Store

Nutrisize Health is designed as a local-first app. Depending on how you use the app, Nutrisize Health may store information on your device, including:

  • Health and profile information you choose to enter (such as age, weight, height, resting heart rate, blood pressure, fasting glucose, body temperature, oxygen saturation, activity level, goals, dietary preferences, medical constraints, and country or region)
  • Saved health history, notes, and reference-related records
  • Grocery items, meal plans, and exercise plans you create
  • Photos captured for grocery or equipment scanning
  • Physiological parameter tracking data
  • In-app notification preferences and any optional contact information
  • Local app settings, zoom preferences, and feature-access state
  • Feedback mood, message text, and device/diagnostic information (transmitted only when you choose to send feedback)
  • A disclaimer-acceptance record — including the disclaimer version and text, the time of acceptance, an anonymous per-installation identifier, and device/diagnostic information (transmitted once when you accept the in-app disclaimer)
  • Limited purchase-status information needed to reflect an eligible App Store or Google Play in-app purchase
  • Local advertising state: your ad-consent choices, how many rewarded videos you have watched today, and ad frequency counters (kept on your device only)

The free parts of the app are supported by advertising (see Section 7). Advertising is the one area where information is collected by a third party, Google; it is described separately so it is easy to find.

4 How We Use Information

Nutrisize Health uses information primarily to provide app features on your device, including:

  • Personalized nutrition guidance, meal planning, and exercise recommendations
  • Physiological parameter browsing, tracking, and health monitoring
  • Saved health history and local reference tracking
  • Grocery inventory management and photo-based scanning
  • Key Performance Indicator (KPI) dashboards for health metrics
  • Local app settings, accessibility (zoom), and purchased feature access

5 Local-First Design

EqualInformation, LLC does not operate a general Nutrisize Health cloud sync service for users' health records and profile data. The health and profile information you enter into Nutrisize Health is intended to remain on your device unless you choose to interact with external third-party services from within the app, unless an ad is requested (Section 7), or unless you choose to send feedback (which transmits only the information described in Section 6).

6 Feedback, Diagnostic, and Disclaimer-Acceptance Data

Nutrisize Health transmits limited technical information to a Google Sheets endpoint operated by EqualInformation, LLC in two situations: (a) when you send feedback, and (b) when you accept the in-app legal disclaimer. In neither case does it include your health records, profile data, name, email, or account credentials.

When you send feedback (only after you tap the "Send Feedback" button):

  • Your selected mood and feedback message text
  • App name, version, platform (iOS/Android/Web), and language settings
  • Device model and manufacturer, and operating system version
  • Hardware details such as chip/SoC, CPU core count and architecture, GPU, and total memory (RAM)
  • Screen and window size, storage usage, app memory, locale/region, and timezone
  • Anonymous app-usage counts (for example, the number of saved health records, meal plans, and exercise plans)

When you accept the disclaimer (transmitted once, automatically, at first launch or after the disclaimer is updated):

  • The disclaimer version, the exact disclaimer text shown to you, and a cryptographic hash of that text
  • The date and time you accepted, whether you scrolled through the text, and how long it was on screen
  • An anonymous, randomly generated per-installation identifier. This is not tied to your name, email, or any account, does not identify you personally, is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking, is separate from the advertising identifier described in Section 7, and resets if you reinstall the app
  • The same device and hardware details listed above

This information is used to improve the app, respond to bug reports, and maintain a record that the legal disclaimer was presented and accepted. All transmission occurs over HTTPS. The endpoint is hosted by Google, which may process technical request information (such as IP address) under Google's own policies.

7 Advertising (Google AdMob), Consent, and Remove Ads

Nutrisize Health is free to download, with optional paid upgrades. The free parts of the app show ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads can be removed with the optional Remove Ads purchase (Section 9), and several other purchases remove ads for the areas they cover.

Where ads appear. Banner ads at the bottom of some long free list screens, occasional full-screen ads at natural pauses (for example after saving a quick log or closing a learning article), and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch to unlock a free item for the day. Ads do not appear on first launch or onboarding, during data entry, on purchase screens, in the Coach and Health areas, on Apple Watch or Wear OS, or in widgets. Ads are not shown for areas you have paid to unlock.

What the ad service receives. When an ad is requested, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier (the Advertising ID on Android; the IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and operating-system details, app and SDK version, language, ad interaction data (such as which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped), and diagnostic and performance data. This information is used to deliver, limit the frequency of, measure, and secure ads, and, where you have allowed it, to personalize them, as described in Google's own policies.

What is never sent to advertisers. Nutrisize Health does not give advertisers or Google AdMob the health, nutrition, exercise, or profile information you enter in the app, your Apple Health or Health Connect data, your photos, or your feedback. The app does not tailor ads using that information, and it does not display ads inside the health-content areas listed above.

Consent. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and some US states), the app shows Google's consent message (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested, and ads are not requested until the choices you make allow it. You can review or change those choices at any time in System > Data & Privacy > Privacy options where your region requires that option. On iOS, the app asks permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, never at first launch, and only shortly before an ad is shown in a later session. You can choose “Ask App Not to Track” and still use every feature; you will still see ads, but they will not use the IDFA.

How to opt out or limit advertising.

  • Buy Remove Ads (one-time purchase), or any purchase or subscription that covers the area you use. Restore Purchases brings it back on a new device.
  • Change your consent choices under System > Data & Privacy > Privacy options (where shown).
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or Settings > Nutrisize Health, to turn off tracking; Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising to limit personalized ads.
  • Android: Settings > Privacy (or Google > Ads) > Ads, to delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization.
  • You can also use the controls at adssettings.google.com.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

8 External Websites, Chat Providers, and Reference Sources

Nutrisize Health may allow you to open or view third-party websites, chat services, or reference materials from within the app. If you choose to use those third-party services, your device may connect directly to those services, and those services may receive information such as your IP address, device, browser or WebView information, and any information you choose to enter. Those third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms, not this Privacy Policy.

9 App Store and Google Play Billing

Nutrisize Health offers optional purchases, including Remove Ads, module unlocks, a premium bundle, and the Coach subscription. Nutrisize Health may use Apple App Store In-App Purchase (on iOS) or Google Play Billing (on Android) for eligible in-app purchase functionality. Apple or Google may process payment and transaction information under their own respective policies. Nutrisize Health may receive limited purchase-status or product information needed to unlock purchased content on your device. EqualInformation, LLC does not receive your full payment card number through Nutrisize Health.

10 Nutrition and Exercise Databases

Nutrisize Health includes bundled nutrition and exercise databases stored locally on your device. The app may optionally fetch additional food data from the USDA FoodData Central API or generate exercise variations locally. These database expansion features, when enabled, connect to external data sources but do not transmit any of your personal or health data.

11 Permissions

Nutrisize Health may request the following device permissions:

  • Camera: Used for grocery and equipment photo scanning (only when you choose to use this feature)
  • Internet: Used for feedback submission, database expansion syncs, in-app purchase verification, and loading ads
  • Advertising ID (Android) and App Tracking (iOS): Used only by the advertising service described in Section 7. On iOS you are asked before the IDFA is used. On Android the app declares the Advertising ID permission, and you can delete or reset the ID in system settings.

Nutrisize Health's core nutrition, exercise, and health features do not require SMS or Call Log permissions. Nutrisize Health is not intended to operate as a default SMS, phone, or assistant application.

12 Sharing and Sale of Data

EqualInformation, LLC does not sell personal or sensitive user data. We do not share users' health data with data brokers or with advertisers, and we do not use it to target ads. Except for user-initiated feedback submissions, the disclaimer-acceptance record described in Section 6, advertising data collected by Google AdMob as described in Section 7, interactions with third-party websites or services, and limited purchase-status handling through App Store or Google Play, the company does not intentionally transmit personal or sensitive Nutrisize Health user data to its own servers or unrelated third parties.

13 Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored by Nutrisize Health generally remains on your device until you delete it inside the app (via Data Management), clear the app's local storage, or uninstall the app. You may export your health data as a JSON file at any time from the Data Management screen. Purchase-related unlock state may also be restorable through App Store or Google Play for eligible purchases. Feedback data and disclaimer-acceptance records submitted to Google Sheets are retained by EqualInformation, LLC for product-improvement and legal record-keeping purposes.

14 Security

Nutrisize Health relies on iOS and Android platform protections, app sandboxing, and on-device storage as part of its local-first design. Feedback submissions are transmitted over HTTPS. However, no method of storage or electronic transmission is completely secure, and the company cannot guarantee absolute security.

15 Your Choices

You may choose what information to enter into Nutrisize Health. You may choose not to use external chat services, browser links, or embedded third-party web content. You may choose not to send feedback. You may decline or change your advertising consent choices, decline tracking on iOS, reset or delete your advertising ID, or remove ads with the optional Remove Ads purchase (see Section 7). You may remove locally stored app data by using the Data Management screen, clearing app storage, or uninstalling the app. You may export your data before deletion.

16 Children

Nutrisize Health is rated for ages 16 and over and is not directed to children; ads are not configured for child-directed treatment. Nutrisize Health is not intended to knowingly collect personal information online from children through an EqualInformation, LLC-operated backend service. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, please contact the company.

17 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the Effective Date above.
